About the Workbooks

Genesis Foundations is a two-part study designed to take you back to the beginning with depth, clarity, and reverence for Scripture. Each workbook is structured for slow study, daily reflection, and intentional return to the text.

Part 1 walks through Genesis 1 with Hebrew word studies, Selah prompts, and guided structure.

Part 2 continues into Genesis 2 with breath, garden, river, prohibition, and covenant union.

Both parts are created to help you study, write, and return.

What You Will Study: Part 1

  • The structure of Genesis 1
  • The Hebrew meaning of “beginning”
  • The creative pattern of God
  • The separation and ordering of creation
  • The purpose of light and darkness
  • The formation of the heavens and the earth
  • The establishment of time, seasons, and days
  • The creation of vegetation and living creatures
  • The image of God in humanity
  • The blessing, mandate, and dominion
  • The completion of creation and the meaning of “very good”

What You Will Study: Part 2

  • The formation of man from the dust
  • The breath of God and the meaning of “living soul”
  • The planting of the Garden in Eden
  • The four rivers and their significance
  • The trees: Life and Knowledge
  • The command, prohibition, and divine boundaries
  • The assignment and work given to Adam
  • The search for a suitable helper
  • The creation of woman from bone
  • The meaning of “bone of my bone”
  • The establishment of covenant union

Who This Study Is For

  • Women who want deeper Bible study
  • Beginners who need guided structure
  • Teachers preparing to lead groups
  • Anyone returning to foundational truth
  • Readers who want slow, intentional study

How to Use the Workbooks

  • Read the passage slowly
  • Use the Hebrew notes for clarity
  • Follow the daily Selah prompts
  • Write your reflections
  • Return to the text before moving on
  • Complete Part 1 before beginning Part 2

Study Tools

Genesis Foundations includes simple, effective tools to help you study Scripture with clarity and depth:

  • Hebrew word notes for key terms
  • Concordance references for deeper understanding
  • Selah prompts for reflection and application
  • Structured daily reading sections
  • Space for writing, observing, and returning
  • Visual guides that outline the flow of Genesis 1 and 2

These tools are designed to support slow, intentional study and help you build a strong biblical foundation.

Study Philosophy

Genesis Foundations is built on a slow-study approach: reading carefully, observing intentionally, and returning repeatedly. The goal is not speed, but depth — allowing Scripture to shape understanding, identity, and devotion.

  • We study with reverence.
  • We prioritize clarity over complexity.
  • We return to the text before forming conclusions.
  • We allow Scripture to interpret Scripture.
  • We build foundations that strengthen faith and purpose.

This study invites women to begin again, at the beginning, with intention and devotion.

Study Commitment

Genesis Foundations is designed for women who are ready to study with intention and consistency. Each day requires slow reading, reflection, and a return to the text.

  • Set aside dedicated time each day.
  • Read the passage before the notes.
  • Write your observations and reflections.
  • Move at the pace of Scripture, not speed.
  • Allow the study to shape understanding and devotion.

This commitment builds the foundation needed for deeper study and long-term growth.

Study Outcomes

By completing Genesis Foundations Part 1 and Part 2, you will:

  • Understand the structure and flow of Genesis 1 and 2
  • Gain clarity on foundational Hebrew terms
  • Build a stronger biblical framework for identity and purpose
  • Develop consistent study habits rooted in slow reading
  • Strengthen your confidence in interpreting Scripture
  • Establish a firm beginning for deeper biblical study

These outcomes help you grow in devotion, understanding, and long-term spiritual maturity.
