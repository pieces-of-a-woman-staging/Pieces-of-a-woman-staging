# Policy copy for business review

These drafts are not linked from the live website. They are based on the current
code and the owner's supplied policies, and need business-practice confirmation
before publication.

## Privacy — verified website behavior

- The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.
- Fonts are requested from Google Fonts.
- Email signup uses Kit, including the embedded form on Get the Book.
- Community links open Zoom and Facebook Messenger.
- Order-inquiry links open the visitor's email app.
- The contact form prepares an email draft in the visitor's email app. It does not
  store messages on the website or report that a message has been delivered.
- The site code reviewed has no advertising pixel, analytics script, or
  first-party cookie/local-storage implementation. This is not a claim that
  external services collect no information.

Draft text:

Pieces Of A Woman Bible Studies receives information you choose to provide when
you send an email, request order or ministry support, or join the email community.
This can include your name, email address, and the content of your message.
Email signup is handled by Kit. Contact and order inquiries use your email app.
The website itself does not store contact-form messages.

GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Google Fonts supplies the website fonts. These
services receive the connection information needed to respond to browser requests.
Zoom, Facebook Messenger, and Kit have their own privacy practices when you use
their services. Review their policies before sharing information.

For privacy questions or requests about information you have supplied, contact
info@piecesofawoman.org.

Before publishing, the owner needs to confirm:

1. How subscriber, inquiry, and order information is used, shared, and retained;
   how deletion and unsubscribe requests are handled.
2. The actual checkout provider and account, and whether any analytics, ad pixels,
   or other services run outside this GitHub codebase.

## Terms — owner-supplied content available to reuse
