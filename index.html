7 DAYS THROUGH GENESIS 1

What You Will Study

✦

Each day focuses on one act of creation — what God did, what the Hebrew says, and what He is revealing about how He deals with His children. Study the concordance. Sit in the Selah. Let God teach.

Day 1

Light — Illumination & Clarity

H-216 · Or — to make clear

Before light: dark, empty, void, no identity, no direction. The condition of the earth mirrors the human heart before salvation. God speaks light and the void cannot remain.

Day 2

Firmament — Structure Before Filling

H-7549 · Raqia — beaten expanse

God hammers space into chaos. The only day He doesn't say "good" — because structure alone isn't good. Structure that leads to fruit is good. He separates BEFORE He fills.

Day 3

Land & Seed — Promise Revealed

H-2233 · Zera — every seed carries its design

Dry land appears only after waters are gathered. Your promise is hidden under what God has to confine first. The only day God says "it was good" twice — double favor.

Day 4

Sun, Moon, Stars — Discern the Season

H-4150 · Moed — appointed times

God sets time before He sets life in motion. Many are anointed but out of season. These lights don't control you — they measure you. Discern your season or be devoured by it.

Day 5

Birds & Fish — First Blessing

H-1288 · Blessed to multiply

The first blessing in all of scripture. Stagnation is not blessable — movement is. Birds fly in the open firmament, not the lower waters. Elevation requires operating in your appointed space.

Day 6

Animals & Man — Five-Fold Authority

H-6754 · Tselem — representative figure

Made in His image. Five commands: fruitful, multiply, replenish, subdue, dominion. You were not made to look like God and act like the devil. You fight FROM dominion — not for it.

Day 7

Rest — Celebrating Completion

H-7673 · Shabath — cease and celebrate

God wasn't tired. He modeled completion. The first holy thing in scripture is not a place — it is a day. Rest is warfare. Striving is doubt with sweat on it.

Deeper Study

Darkness, Evening & Morning, Light Connections

H-2822 · Choshek — obscurity

Three deeper sections go beyond the seven days: what darkness really means in Hebrew, why God counts days from evening to morning, and how light connects God, His word, and His children.

THE ASSIGNMENT

Sappreha Darby

TEACHER • INTERCESSOR • STUDENT OF THE WORD

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Pieces Of A Woman Bible Studies was not built from ambition. It was built from assignment.

Vonita Geddins - my aunt - spent eight years studying Genesis structurally. She filled notebook after notebook tracing Hebrew words back to the beginning: plain meaning, spiritual meaning, no opinions, no overreaches. Just return to what God established first.

She told me: “Share this information. I’m teaching teachers.” She passed the notebooks not to be kept, but to be continued.

Irma Geddins - my mother - created Pieces Of A Woman Ministry. I created Pieces Of A Woman Bible Studies from what she built and what Vonita poured. The body forgot the beginning.

This workbook series exists to change that.

“I am not her student. I am her assignment.”

“The same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.” — 2 Timothy 2:2, KJV

A Special Dedication

Honoring Auntie Vonita Geddins

Her notebooks. Her teaching. The foundation behind Genesis Foundations.

The Notebooks That Started It

Where the foundation began

Genesis Foundations is rooted in the notebooks of my aunt, Vonita Geddins. For eight years, she studied Genesis structurally, filling notebook after notebook as she traced Hebrew words back to the beginning.

Her study returned to what God established first: plain meaning, spiritual meaning, no opinions, and no overreaches.

She passed those notebooks to me to be continued and shared. Her work is the foundation behind this workbook series, and this space honors her teaching and the notebooks she entrusted to me.

— Vonita Geddins
